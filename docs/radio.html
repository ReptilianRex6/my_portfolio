Amateur Radio · KM7CPJ

RADIO & SIGNALS

I enjoy the strange magic of sending a signal into a wire and having another human hear it hundreds—or thousands—of miles away.

Why Radio?

Amateur radio combines electronics, networking, emergency readiness, experimentation, and community service. It scratches the same itch as software: understand the system, reduce noise, and make a reliable connection.

Licensed General-class amateur radio operator. Callsign: KM7CPJ.

Current Interests

  • HF voice and digital communication
  • Parks on the Air portable operation
  • Satellite and ISS contacts
  • ARES and emergency communications
  • Antenna construction and tuning
  • Meshtastic and off-grid data networks

Radio Bench

Primary Station

Portable Operations

Digital & Mesh
