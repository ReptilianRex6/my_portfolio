About

I spent seven years programming Fanuc machines before I started taking software work. The habit carried over: diagnose carefully, write the setup down, and prefer the version that still works after the demo.

Freelance web projects are the work I can show end to end. At Lava Studios I work on Aisthetic, a generative image and video product, mostly on the service and cloud side.

I hold a General-class amateur radio license, KM7CPJ, and I train Brazilian jiu-jitsu.
