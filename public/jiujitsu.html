Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu

PRESSURE & PROBLEM SOLVING

Jiu-jitsu is live troubleshooting: every plan meets resistance, every position gives feedback, and ego causes remarkably reproducible bugs.

What It Teaches Me

Training reinforces habits that carry directly into engineering: stay calm while something is failing, work from fundamentals, conserve energy, adapt quickly, and never confuse confidence with proof.

The goal is not to know every technique. It is to recognize the problem, protect what matters, and improve the position one decision at a time.

Lessons From the Mat

  • Composure under pressure
  • Iterative problem solving
  • Learning through immediate feedback
  • Respect for fundamentals
  • Humility and continuous improvement
  • Supporting teammates

Why It Belongs Here

Engineering Mindset

A failed approach is data. Reset, identify the constraint, and try a cleaner sequence instead of muscling through a broken plan.

Long-Term Practice

Progress comes from consistent repetitions, good coaching, honest review, and accepting that sometimes a smaller person is about to teach you physics.
