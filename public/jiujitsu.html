Brazilian jiu-jitsu

Stay calm. Solve the next problem.

Live troubleshooting. Every plan meets resistance, and ego causes bugs you can reproduce.

Current rank Always learning

One stripe at a time. Plenty left to learn.

What it teaches me

Stay calm while something is failing. Work from fundamentals. Conserve energy. Adapt. Do not confuse confidence with proof.

The goal is not to know every technique. It is to recognize the problem, protect what matters, and improve the position one decision at a time.

From the mat

  • Composure under pressure
  • Iterative problem solving
  • Learning from immediate feedback
  • Respect for fundamentals
  • Humility, on a schedule
  • Supporting teammates

A failed approach is data

Reset, find the constraint, and try a cleaner sequence. Muscling through a broken plan is how you get tired and still lose.

Long practice

Progress is repetitions, coaching, and honest review. Sometimes a smaller person is about to teach you physics.
