Amateur radio · KM7CPJ

A signal in a wire, and another person on the other end.

Licensed General-class operator. Callsign KM7CPJ. The web tools around the hobby have their own note.

KM7CPJ on QRZ Radio tools note

Why radio

It mixes electronics, antennas, emergency readiness, and a community that will tell you when your signal is bad. That last part is the same as software: reduce the noise and make a connection you can count on.

The station today is a Yaesu FT-991A, an antenna analyzer, a tuner, and whatever wire I am trying this month. Portable work is a battery, a compact kit, and a plan for Parks on the Air. Digital experiments include FreeDV, packet, and Meshtastic nodes for when the internet is not the path.

The vertical in the photo is the station antenna right now: a guyed mast on a base plate, with the guys tied off in the grass.

Current interests

  • HF voice and digital
  • Parks on the Air
  • Satellite and ISS contacts
  • ARES and emergency communications
  • Antenna building and tuning
  • Meshtastic and off-grid data
